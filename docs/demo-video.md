# Demo video plan

A shot list and script for the competition video. RUSH is its face: every
shot either starts or ends on RUSH reacting.

Check the official rules before recording, because this plan could not read
them: https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/rules. Confirm the
maximum length, where the video must be hosted (for example a public or
unlisted YouTube link), and whether it must show the app running on the
device. The plan below aims for 2 minutes, which fits the 3-minute limit most
Devpost competitions use; cut the optional shots if the limit is shorter.

## What the video must prove

1. Hands only: no controller appears anywhere, menus included.
2. Seated: everything happens within arm's reach of a chair.
3. Quick to get: a stranger understands a puzzle in a few seconds.
4. RUSH: a character with a personality who is also the clock.
5. A reason to come back: the Daily Challenge and its streak.

## Before recording

- Build: the live site (https://oreghun.github.io/thinkrush-vr/) without
  `?fps`. Record only after the fps check on the Quest shows 72 fps, so the
  footage is smooth.
- Headset: Quest 3, hand tracking on, controllers put away out of reach so
  they never wake up. Good, even room light helps hand tracking.
- Recording: in the headset's Camera settings, pick landscape (16:9) video
  if it is offered. Start and stop a recording from the universal menu
  (look at your palm and pinch the Meta icon, then Camera, Record video).
  Turn the microphone off: the voiceover is added in the edit.
- Game settings on the home page: Read aloud Normal, Sound on, English.
- Moves: a device that has seen fewer than three visual puzzles only asks
  for DROP. To show the move twist, play three visual puzzles first (or use
  a device that already has). Clearing the browser's site data starts the
  count over, and also clears streaks and best scores.
- Sit in a real chair, facing forward, and keep head movement slow: viewers
  watch through your eyes, and fast turns are hard to watch.

## Shot list (about 2 minutes)

Times are targets for the final cut. Record each shot as its own take, two
or three times, and pick the best in the edit.

If the video has to be shorter, keep shots 1, 3, 4, 5 and 9 (about 75
seconds).

## End card text

- ThinkRush VR
- Hands only. Seated. Daily puzzles.
- Play at https://oreghun.github.io/thinkrush-vr/

Use only the game's own sound (its music and RUSH's voice are synthesized in
the game) or no music at all. No stock music, logos or brand names, to stay
within the originality rules. Add English captions that match the voiceover.

## Claims to keep honest

- "Built for Meta VR Glasses" is backed by the emulator check (the view fits,
  gaze and pinch work). Do not show or describe a real pair of glasses unless
  one was tested.
- Say 72 fps or 60 fps only after the `?fps` readout on the Quest shows it.
